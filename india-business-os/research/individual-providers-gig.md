## 2. Market ground truth
- **Size:** gig+platform workers ≈ **12M (FY25)**, ~2% of workforce; → 23.5M / 6.7% non-agri + ₹2.35 lakh cr GDP by 2029-30 `[VERIFIED — Econ Survey/NITI]`. Home services online supply is tiny: **~0.1M workers today → 0.2–0.3M by 2030** vs a market that is **>98% offline** `[VERIFIED — Redseer]`.
- **Earnings:** platform gig internet workers ≈ **₹138/hr ≈ 2.5× informal** (₹54/hr); home services full-time ≈ ₹70–80k/mo net; all-cohort avg ≈ ₹32k/mo (mostly part-time) `[VERIFIED — Redseer]`. But **40% of gig workers earn <₹15k/mo**; income volatile; "thin-file" credit `[VERIFIED — Econ Survey]`. UC: avg net ₹26–34k/mo; top 5% ≈ ₹50k but only via **hours** (flat ₹310-313/hr) `[VERIFIED — UC data via Mint]`.
- **Worker-side pain (the opportunity):** upfront **lead fees (₹40–250/lead) + ₹3,000/mo subscriptions** regardless of conversion; commission erosion over time (partner example: ₹55–60k/mo → ₹13k; service ₹1,400 → ₹800); no upward mobility; women: same monthly pay as men despite higher hourly (fewer hours, safety, transport ₹9–12k/mo); demand-predictability is the #1 retention driver `[VERIFIED — Mint/Forbes/NASSCOM-Foundation analysis via Mid-Day]`.

## 3. Regulation & trust reality for individuals
- **No premises-licence regime** applies (no S&E/trade licence for a plumber working from home) → our shop-pack licence journey does NOT fit individuals. Their formal stack: Udyam/Udyam Assist (free, credit gate), GST above threshold (services ₹20L), e-Shram registration (gig/social-security), sector skill certs (ITI/NSDC; UC trains + certifies via NSDC).
- **Karnataka Gig Act 2025** (in force from 30-May-2025, notified 12-Sep-2025): if our product becomes an "aggregator/platform" matching individual providers in KA → register with Welfare Board, worker registration + portable Unique IDs, **1–5% welfare fee per payout**, PWFVS transaction reporting, weekly payouts, fair contracts (14-day change notice), IDRC (>50 workers), no adverse action on ratings alone `[VERIFIED]`. Rajasthan (2023) & Bihar (2025) Acts; Jharkhand draft. **Update (2026-09-08): Telangana Platform-Based Gig Workers Act 2026 — passed 30-Mar-2026, assented 1-May-2026, in force ~15-Aug-2026: Welfare Board, worker unique IDs, aggregator registration within 45 days, 1–2% levy on transaction value, quarterly electronic returns, IDRC ≥100 workers, penalties ₹50k/₹1L/₹1.5L → 5× arrears `[VERIFIED — Telangana Today/ANI 30-Mar-2026; Deccan Chronicle 4-Aug-2026]`** → both KA and TS now regulate platform work; per-state obligation depth differs (KA 1–5% vs TS 1–2%), so the legal gate is per-state, not one-off. → **Legal watch item before onboarding individuals as a marketplace in EITHER pilot state.**
- **Trust infrastructure today:** platforms substitute for verification (UC training/kit; Pyng "verified"; HelpRush KYC+trust ledger). Public rails emerging: e-Shram UAN, Karnataka portable worker ID, PM Vishwakarma (craftspeople skilling/support; HelpRush onboarded via a Directorate of Industries letter) `[VERIFIED]`.

## 5. Product implications for India Business OS
1. **Tiered trust, not one badge.** Shops get the existing compliance badge (registrations valid). Individuals get a **ProBadge** = identity-verified (Aadhaar/photo), skill certificate(s), references, optional police verification, live order-verified ratings. Mixing these semantics under one badge would break LM2-style trust audits → keep two derived flags on the same profile (`kind: business|individual`).
2. **Person packs = config on the same core** (like vertical packs): onboarding flow = ID+skill verification + availability + catalog-of-services, not licence journeys. Recommended **wave C** (post-pilot) — with a legal-review gate before we function as a matching marketplace in KA (Gig Act obligations). Until then, individual trades inside pilot verticals ride the shop/profile model with a "mobile service" flag.
3. **Our wedge ≠ another home-services aggregator** (UC full-stack / Pyng / instant apps). Those monetize supply (fees/commissions) and hide the pro. The market is signalling the opposite direction (ProNearMe zero-upfront, HelpRush public pro pages) + worker demand for predictable earnings and **ownership of the customer relationship**. Our order-linked, no-upfront, badge-driven model aligns with that — differentiation statement for the docs.
4. **Provider-side value props (from research):** badge = trust credential that *travels* (customers keep rebooking the same pro — HelpRush/UC-direct-rebooking evidence); earnings predictability (no lead packs — aligns with our no-lead-selling guardrail); tools (quotes, invoices, scheduling, UPI collection); safety framing for women providers (visible verification both ways).
5. **Numbers for the pitch:** home services >98% offline & ~$50-60B; online supply ~0.1M pros; workers earn 2.5× informal on platforms yet 40% <₹15k/mo; trust is the category bottleneck — exactly what a verified-local layer sells.

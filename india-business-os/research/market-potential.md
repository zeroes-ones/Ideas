## 1. Top-down TAM (context, not plan)
- **India SaaS:** $7.18B (2024) → ~$80B (2034), 27.3% CAGR `[VERIFIED — R&M Aug-2025]`; India domestic software $20B → $100B by 2035 with **SMB vertical SaaS ≈ $13B slice** `[VERIFIED — SaaSBoomi+1Lattice Mar-2025]`. We are a vertical-SaaS wedge inside that slice (local-commerce compliance+trust).
- **Business universe:** Udyam+Assist registrations 6.3 Cr+ · active GST registrations 1.52 Cr · kirana 13M (≈1.4M digitised) `[VERIFIED — market-deep-dive D4; AICPDF]`. Formalization is accelerating: GST e-invoicing mandatory ≤₹2 Cr turnover from Oct-2025; UPI + 709M QR codes pulling the informal into the formal economy `[VERIFIED — Commmerce/AICPDF Jul-2026]`.
- **Theoretical TAM frame (honest):** the addressable *subscription* market is not 6.3 Cr MSMEs (most are unregistered/micro with no licence calendar) but the **registered/formalizing, premises-based businesses** (rungs XS→M): ~1.5–2.5 Cr `[ESTIMATED]`. At ₹1,200–3,600/yr that is a **₹24,000–72,000 Cr/yr theoretical envelope** — a ceiling, never a target.
- **Local-discovery monetization already exists at scale** (the market *pays*, just for the wrong thing): Justdial FY26 **₹1,213.9 Cr revenue, 6.3L active paid campaigns** (54.7M listings; traffic declining −4.7% YoY — a trust-saturated incumbent) · IndiaMART FY26 **₹1,569 Cr** `[VERIFIED — NSE filings FY26]`. **≈₹1.6k/mo per Justdial campaign** is the single best proof that a kirana-class merchant will pay for *local demand* — our ladder top (₹299/mo) undercuts it ~5×.

## 4. "If executed correctly" — the gates that make or break the numbers
1. **WTP gate:** H4 interviews → median accepted ₹ ≥ ₹99/mo and tier contents ranked (calendar vs badge vs storefront). *Kills SOM if <₹99.*
2. **Cold-start gate:** ≥100 verified merchants *per cluster* before consumers (playbook W35–W40) — supply density, not spread.
3. **Retention gate:** the renewals calendar must re-prove value monthly (churn <5%/mo; SM7–SM8). SaaS math dies on churn, not CAC.
4. **Graduation gate:** I→XS+ conversions convert free individuals into paying businesses (the ₹0 tier's monetization path).
5. **Trust gate:** LM2 badge integrity 100% — one public "badge shown on non-compliant shop" story costs a year of trust (marketplace-audit §2).
6. **Cost gate:** two-city parallel ops ≈ 2× supply-ramp cost; per-cluster ≥10–15 density before scaling (Gap A) — else CAC eats the ₹150/mo.
7. **Config gate:** city/state packs must make BLR→HYD→other cities cheap (the architecture bet).

**Net whitespace statement (one line for a partner):** *every incumbent owns one layer (leads, filings, billing, ads, rail) and none owns the loop — compliance → live badge → verified demand; we monetize the one thing no one sells fairly: the calendar that keeps a business legitimate and the customers that legitimacy brings.*

## 2. OW — what business owners are looking for

| ID | Owner want | Evidence | Our lever |
|---|---|---|---|
| OW1 | **More customers / growth without complexity** | 60% rely on WhatsApp Business for sales+marketing; 76% say losing it = serious setback `[VERIFIED — India SME Forum]`; 41.4% of digitised MSMEs saw 21–30% sales rise; 69% grew customer base ≥10% `[VERIFIED — n=7,835]` | Discovery + storefront (D1/D2) as an *additional* channel next to WhatsApp, not a replacement |
| OW2 | **Simple, integrated, mobile-first tools** (no tool sprawl) | 52.6% struggle to identify the right tool `[VERIFIED]`; experts: fragmentation (CRM/billing/comms in silos) is the core problem `[VERIFIED — multi-vendor]`; smartphones = business device for 71% (84% women) `[VERIFIED — PayNearby]` | One guided profile → modules activate (C3); no app-store shopping for owners |
| OW3 | **Being found & trusted digitally** (beyond WhatsApp) | 40% of registered nano-enterprises say customers ask for proof of registration before buying; lack of customer trust = key cost of informality `[VERIFIED — IFMR LEAD]`; 71–73% worry about losing ad targeting on Google/FB `[VERIFIED]` | Verified badge (B1) = the digital "proof of registration" customers already ask for |
| OW4 | **Handholding through compliance — not more paperwork** | Multiple registration points create confusion/inertia; owners register with human assistance (28% ready to register *with support*) `[VERIFIED — IFMR LEAD]`; compliance costs/technostress cited as heavy (43% of MSME staff lose time to portals, per Stratefix in DQ) `[VERIFIED — vendor claim, directional]` | Launch Copilot guided journeys + honest govt-vs-professional fee framing (C2) |
| OW5 | **Fear of aggressive enforcement & complex returns** | Karnataka UPI→GST notices risk pushing traders back informal (SBI Research) `[VERIFIED]`; nano firms say GST costs often outweigh benefits just above threshold `[VERIFIED — Deasra]` | Compliance-as-growth (badge/credit), never fear-based messaging; transparent "when you must vs may" (C1/C5) |
| OW6 | **Margin & working-capital relief** | 89% of kiranas seek better trade margins; 45% ask credit support `[VERIFIED — CPM India]`; credit gap widens as formality rises (borrowing ↑ with registration) `[VERIFIED — NSS 73rd via The Wire]` | Udyam/UAP as the credit gate (free, day-1); content + later fintech partnerships (O9) |
| OW7 | **Vernacular, low-friction UI** | 56% prefer English; 38% of women prefer local-language interfaces `[VERIFIED — PayNearby]` | EN+Hindi core (already a requirement); more languages later |
| OW8 | **Know what's available (schemes, tools)** | 97.3% unaware of existing government digitalisation schemes `[VERIFIED — n=7,835]` | ContentBlocks surfacing relevant schemes per profile (C-series) |

**Owner-side bottom line:** owners don't want *more* software — they want **customers, trust, and simpler compliance**, delivered through one tool that rides their existing habits (WhatsApp, UPI, phone-first). Barriers are cost, training, complexity, and fear of scrutiny — not absence of desire (73% report growth/efficiency from digital adoption `[VERIFIED — PayNearby]`).

## 4. GAP matrix — where owner reality and customer want do not meet

| Gap | Owner reality | Customer want | Bridge (module) | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 **Trust gap** | Formalised but invisible; badge-equivalent doesn't exist for small shops | Wants proof the shop is real/compliant (40% already ask) | Verified badge auto-derived from compliance state (B1, C3) | P0 — the whole two-sided loop |
| G2 **Discovery gap** | Reach = footfall + WhatsApp + paid ads they fear losing | Finds shops via QC apps or luck; wants nearby, verified options | Discovery + storefront + vertical filter (D1/D2); supply = owners we formalise (LM1) | P0 |
| G3 **Deal gap** | Cannot create/run offers; discounts happen verbally, untracked | 47% wait for discounts; wants genuine local offers | Merchant deals engine, server-validated (D8/B3) | P0 |
| G4 **Ordering gap** | POS/inventory/digital-ordering adoption low (cost/complexity) though UPI standard `[V]`; only 22% plan tech spend `[V]` | Urgent top-ups + routine orders with zero-queue convenience | Catalog + order bridge, pickup/self-delivery (D3/D4); phone-first, no hardware | P0 |
| G5 **Service-booking gap** | Appointment trades (salon/garage/repair/coaching) run on call/missed calls | Wants slot certainty + price clarity before visiting | Availability slots + booking + confirm/decline (D9); pay-at-shop default | P0 |
| G6 **Compliance-fear gap** | Fears paperwork, scrutiny, costs of formalising | (Indirect) wants the *benefits* of formal shops — trust, quality, recourse | Guided journeys + honest framing + customer-led formalisation pull (C2 + badge) | P0/P1 |
| G7 **Language gap** | 38% of women owners prefer vernacular `[V]` | Hindi/regional UI expected by Bharat users | EN+Hindi core (requirement); i18n ContentBlocks | P1 |
| G8 **Credit-linkage gap** | 45% want credit support; unaware Udyam unlocks PSL | — | Udyam day-1 + scheme ContentBlocks (O9/C-series) | P1 |

## 5. Design implications (guardrails for the product)

1. **Never compete with quick commerce on speed; compete on trust + proximity + price.** Redseer: ₹100–200 AOV mass market cannot pay 18–22% logistics — our model (pickup/self-delivery from real shops) keeps that moat `[VERIFIED]`. QC is for urgency (45%), we are for *routine + trust-based* purchases (kirana anchor) `[VERIFIED]`.
2. **Badge = digital "proof of registration" the customer already demands.** IFMR recommends a *consumer-led* formalization strategy `[VERIFIED]` — external validation of our Entry-B pull loop (customer sees badge → prefers shop → owner formalises to get badge → more customers).
3. **Compliance tone = enablement, never fear.** SBI Research + Deasra warn enforcement-fear drives de-formalisation `[VERIFIED]`; our compliance marketing must lead with badge/credit/trust benefits.
4. **Integrate with WhatsApp behaviour, don't replace it.** 60–76% dependence `[VERIFIED]` → merchant notifications must arrive where owners already are (WhatsApp-forward UX for the business side, P1).
5. **Deals are a *local-shop* feature gap, not a discounting gimmick** — 47% of customers wait for deals, but 86% of kiranas cite customer-shift as their top challenge with no offer tooling `[VERIFIED]`.

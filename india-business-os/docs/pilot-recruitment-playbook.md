# Pilot Recruitment Playbook — Reaching the ≥100 Verified-Merchant Gate (per city: Bengaluru + Hyderabad, parallel)

> **Date:** 2026-09-08 · **Status:** Operational plan answering scope-brief **OQ2** (supply ramp) + gate for Entry B consumer launch (`vision.md` LM1/LM2). Grounded in: `research/bengaluru-pilot-map.md` (denominator/wards) · `research/two-sided-wants-gaps.md` (owner wants, OW) · `research/market-deep-dive.md` (competitive rails). Execute as work items **W35–W40**.

## 1. Objective & numbers
- Gate: **≥100 verified merchants** in-pilot (verified = `formalizationState ≥ registered` ∧ no expired licences) before consumer launch `[scope-brief]`.
- Denominator is *computable*: OpenCity BBMP trade-licence CSVs (ward-level, 28 constituencies) mapped to the 4 verticals (W35) + field sample to correct coverage (W36). Illustrative: a 3-ward cluster at ~300–600 licensed premises/ward and ~30–45% in-vertical gives ~270–800 eligible shops `[ESTIMATED — replace with W35 output]`.
- Feasibility logic: IFMR LEAD found **28% of informal micro-enterprises register with handholding support** `[VERIFIED]`; digitised MSMEs report +21–30% sales / +≥10% customer base `[VERIFIED — n=7,835]` → with active recruitment, ≥100 from one cluster is a planning assumption to validate in W40.

## 2. Who we recruit (from W35/W36)
1. **Licensed shops in-vertical** (OpenCity list) — fastest path: likely already have S&E/trade licence; need badge = add GST/Udyam as applicable + set catalog.
2. **Unlicensed but formalisable shops** found in field walk — need Launch Copilot journey first (Entry A value).
3. **Referrals** — every recruited owner names 2–3 neighbouring shops (kirana clusters are dense; Gumla finding: trust travels through personal networks `[VERIFIED]`).

## 3. Value props — enablement framing only (never fear)
| Prop | Message (grounded stat) |
|---|---|
| Badge = customers | **40% of customers already ask shops for proof of registration** `[IFMR VERIFIED]` — badge answers that before they ask. "Your licence becomes your shopfront." |
| Orders without app fees | Kirana economics: ₹100–200 baskets can't pay 18–22% platform logistics `[Redseer VERIFIED]` — pickup/self-delivery keeps you the cheapest channel; we don't take a cut at launch. |
| Deals tool | **47% of customers wait for discounts** `[Rukam VERIFIED]` — you can finally run offers like the big apps, without an ad budget. |
| Booking slots | Service trades (mechanic/appliance/electrical): customers want **fixed-time slots + price clarity** (validation H3) — stop missed calls. |
| Udyam = credit door | Free, day-1; unlocks MSME credit/PSL (45% of kiranas ask for credit support `[CPM VERIFIED]`). |
| Anti-fear note | "We are not the GST department. We never file on your behalf; we show you the official steps and you stay in control." (KA UPI→GST sensitivity `[SBI VERIFIED]`) |

## 4. Outreach channels & kit (W39)
**Channel mix by expected yield:** door-to-door in W36-sampled streets (highest trust) → WhatsApp broadcast to OpenCity list → shop-association/shutter-owner referrals (ask every owner for 2–3 names).

**WhatsApp template (bilingual EN/HI, send after a visit or listing pull):**
> Namaste [Name]-ji. We are building [Product] to help [vertical] shops in [ward/cluster] get more customers — for free during the pilot. Customers near you will see your shop as **Verified & Compliant** (GST/trade licence checked), can order for pickup or **book a service slot**, and you can post **weekly offers** that 47% of shoppers actively wait for. Setup: your registrations + a phone. No commission at launch. May I send you the 5-line details? — [Your name], [phone]
> (Hindi: नमस्ते [नाम]-जी। हम [प्रोडक्ट] बना रहे हैं ताकि [वर्टिकल] दुकानों को पास के ग्राहक मिलें — पायलट में मुफ़्त। आपकी दुकान **Verified & Compliant** बैज के साथ दिखेगी, ग्राहक पिकअप के लिए ऑर्डर या **सर्विस स्लॉट बुक** कर सकेंगे, और आप **हफ़्ते के ऑफ़र** चला सकेंगे। पायलट में कोई कमीशन नहीं। क्या मैं 5 पंक्तियाँ भेजूँ?)

**Visit script:** 3 questions (licence state, current tools, biggest customer problem) → demo badge card + one deal they could run tomorrow → ask referral. Keep to 7 min; never sell compliance as paperwork.

## 5. Funnel & tracking (W40)
Columns tracked weekly per ward: **approached → interested (said yes to details) → journey started (Udyam/S&E/GST as applicable) → verified (badge live)**.
- Planning conversion (to validate): approached→interested 30–50% (handholding context `[IFMR 28% readiness]` as floor) · interested→started 60–80% · started→verified 70–90% (guided journeys). → 3 wards × ~200 in-vertical shops × realistic cascade ⇒ verify ≥100 is ~3–5 weeks of active recruiting `[ESTIMATED — W40 output]`.
- Weekly board answers LM1 (≥40% of orders from app-onboarded merchants by month 3) and flags stalls by vertical/ward.

## 6. Sequencing
W35 (denominator, wk 1) → W36 (field sample, wk 1–2, doubles as first door-to-door) → W37 (portal confirmation, wk 1) → W38 interviews (wk 1–3; results may re-order value props) → W39 kit + funnel (wk 2) → W40 ramp projection reviewed weekly until ≥100 reached.

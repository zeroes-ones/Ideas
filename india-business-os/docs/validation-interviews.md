# Validation Interview Scripts — Merchants & Consumers (two-sided)

> **Date:** 2026-09-08 · **Status:** Draft v1 for founder use · Method: qualitative, hypothesis-driven, grounded in `research/two-sided-wants-gaps.md` (G1–G8, OW/CW) and `market-deep-dive.md`.
> **Purpose:** resolve scope-brief OQ3 (which hook drives first orders: badge vs deals vs bookings), OQ9 (post-decision validation: **monthly SaaS price point + tier contents** — model already resolved SaaS-first from business owners, 2026-09-08), and feed OQ4/OQ5 (booking readiness, deal/no-show rules).
> **Recommended samples:** ≥10 merchants **per city** (across the 4 pilot verticals; ≥5 in the chosen ward cluster of each city — Bengaluru and Hyderabad in parallel; mix EN/Hindi, mix licensed/unlicensed) · ≥10 consumers **per city** (pilot catchment, UPI-native, mixed genders/ages 25–55). Run 1:1, 25–35 min, in the respondent's language; record with consent (audio → notes only, no PII retained).

## 0. Hypotheses under test
| # | Hypothesis | Evidence if true | Metric link |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Verified badge converts (consumer prefers compliant shop) | Consumer picks badged shop in choice test; cites proof/trust | CM1/CM2, LM2 |
| H2 | Deals pull first orders (esp. grocery) | Deals shown first in shopping-task; merchant eager to run offers | CM6 |
| H3 | Service bookings wanted for mechanic/appliance/electrical | Consumer would book a slot; merchant can keep a calendar | CM4/CM5 |
| H4 | Merchants will pay a **recurring monthly subscription** once value is concrete (calendar = no missed filings/fines; badge/storefront = growth) | Merchant picks a monthly ₹ at/above a viable band in the ladder; ranks calendar vs badge vs storefront contents | SM funnel, sub WTP |
| H5 | WhatsApp is the workflow centre | Merchant says "update me on WhatsApp"; would forward/confirm there | — (design input) |
| H6 | UPI-GST fear is real in KA → enablement framing works | Merchant reacts positively to "badge/credit/customers" framing, negatively to audit framing | C5/C2 tone |

# Script A — Merchant (Entry A/B owner)

**Screener:** owns/operates shop ≤10 staff in {kirana, appliance & electronics, electrical, auto-mechanic} in the launch-city cluster (Bengaluru/Hyderabad; or Entry-A state for founder batch). Exclude: chain/franchise managers.

### A1. Warm-up & current state (5 min)
1. Tell me about your shop — how long, who helps you run it, who are your regular customers?
2. What do you use day-to-day — phone, UPI, any app for billing/khata (Vyapar/OkCredit/other)? *(observe: WhatsApp first?)*
3. Are you registered — Udyam, GST, trade licence, shop act? *(if no)* Why not — what stopped you? *(listen for: fear, cost, "no benefit", confusion — map to OW4/OW5)*

### A2. Growth & pain (8 min)
4. How do new customers find you today — word of mouth, Google, Justdial, WhatsApp groups, passing footfall?
5. *(Show stat: 47% of customers wait for deals; 40% of customers ask shops for proof of registration)* What do you make of that — does that match your customers?
6. What's the single hardest thing about growing this shop right now? *(probe: customers, trust vs big apps, cash/margin, compliance fear — rank)*

### A3. Concept cards (10 min) — show one at a time, in order BADGE → DEALS → ORDERS/BOOKING
- **Badge card:** "Your shop gets a 'Verified & Compliant' badge customers see when they search nearby. Badge shows only while your registrations are valid."
  7. Would you want this? What would it be worth to you? *(probe: "prove to customers you're genuine" vs "govt watching me" reaction → H1/H6)*
  8. Would you complete registrations you're missing *to get the badge*? Which ones would you do first? *(map to OW1/OW3; note licence list order)*
- **Deals card:** "You create offers — ₹20 off on ₹200 grocery, or 10% off service — valid for a week, only in your area."
  9. Would you run such offers? How often? What would stop you (margin, complexity)? *(→ H2, deal rules input for OQ5)*
  10. Where would you want to manage this — phone app or WhatsApp? *(→ H5)*
- **Orders/Booking card:** "Customers order for pickup, or book a service slot (e.g., 'AC check Saturday 11am') — you confirm or decline."
  11. Do you already take orders on WhatsApp/calls? Would slots work for you, or is 'call me first' better? *(→ H3; OQ4)*
  12. For goods, pickup vs your own delivery vs courier — what do you prefer? *(OQ7)*
- **Payment question (goods):** "Customer pays by UPI when ordering." Any concern vs pay-on-pickup? *(probe trust/COD habit)*

### A4. Price test (5 min) — H4 (monthly SaaS ladder; model already decided SaaS-first — do not re-open journey-vs-sub)
13. "Imagine all of this works — verified badge, offers, orders — and the app also reminds you *before* any licence/filing/renewal is due, so you never miss a deadline or pay a fine. Would you pay a **monthly subscription**? Look at this ladder: ₹49 / ₹99 / ₹149 / ₹199 / ₹299 per month — which feels fair, which feels too much, at which would you definitely refuse?" *(record anchors: fair / too-much / refuse + the highest ₹ they'd accept)*
14. "What would the monthly fee have to include to feel worth it — (a) reminders so you never miss a filing/fine, (b) the verified badge customers see, (c) a storefront where customers order and you post offers, or (d) all three together?" *(rank → tier design)*
15. *(If reluctant)* What *would* you pay for — more customers, or less paperwork? How much?
16. *(Fallback signal only, plan-B)* "If a monthly fee is a firm no, which would work instead: pay once at setup, or a small % only when an actual order happens?"

### A5. Close
17. What's missing from what I showed? Who else should I talk to (association, other shopkeepers nearby)?
*Note: thank + offer follow-up; log shop vertical, licence status, digital tools used.*

# Script B — Consumer (Entry B customer)

**Screener:** 25–55, buys daily essentials/services in the launch city (Bengaluru/Hyderabad); has ordered online (QC or e-comm) at least once in 3 months; not an owner of the pilot shops.

### B1. Behaviour today (5 min)
1. Where do you buy groceries/essentials — kirana, supermarket, quick commerce? Rough split? *(observe QC habit)*
2. Last time you needed a mechanic/appliance repair/electrical help, how did you find them — Google, Justdial, building WhatsApp group, asking neighbours? Was it a good experience? *(probe trust/fear of overcharge)*
3. What makes you *avoid* a local shop (or choose one)? *(probe: unsure if genuine, price unknown, no time, no parking)*

### B2. Trust & badge (8 min) — H1/H6
4. *(Show stat: >40% of shops aren't findable online; many claims on Justdial are fake)* Have you been misled finding a service? Tell me about it.
5. *(Badge card: "Registered shop — GST/trade licence verified — shown only while valid")* If two identical shops were near you, one with this badge and one without, which would you choose and why? *(probe: does it feel like 'proof' or like marketing? → H1)*
6. Would you pay a small premium or travel a little further for a badged shop? How much further (streets / km)?

### B3. Deals pull (6 min) — H2/CM6
7. Do you wait for discounts before buying? *(47% stat prompt if needed)* Where do you usually find offers — apps, WhatsApp, shop windows?
8. If your nearby shops posted genuine short-validity offers ("₹20 off on ₹200 grocery this week; 10% off service"), would you check them? How often? *(frequency probe → CM6)*
9. What would make you distrust a "deal" from a local shop? *(probe: inflated MRP, hidden conditions → deal-rule design)*

### B4. Booking services (6 min) — H3/CM5
10. Would you book a mechanic/AC-repair/electrician slot with a fixed time, vs calling and hoping? What matters — confirmation speed, price upfront, or "pay only when done"?
11. Have you ever been stood up (they didn't come / you waited)? How did it change your behaviour? *(no-show policy input)*

### B5. Price & wrap (5 min)
12. Apps charge shops fees which can raise your prices — would you rather see shops pay ₹0 and you pay small delivery/pickup fees, or keep free ordering for you? *(economics signal)*
13. Anything a local shop could do to earn your regular business that online apps can't?

## Debrief template (after each session)
| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Respondent | M/C · vertical/segment · language · licence-state/tools (merchant) |
| H1 badge | chose-badged? reaction (proof vs marketing)? premium tolerance |
| H2 deals | wants deals? frequency? distrust triggers |
| H3 bookings | slot vs call-first; confirmation/price expectations |
| H4 price | monthly-ladder: fair/too-much/refuse + highest accepted ₹; tier ranking (calendar/badge/storefront); fallback signal |
| H5 WhatsApp | where they'd manage/confirm (in-app vs WhatsApp) |
| H6 framing | enablement vs audit reaction |
| Surprises | verbatim quotes worth keeping |

**Exit criteria (read after n≥10/side):** H1/H2/H3 each ≥60% positive with a clear reason; H4: median highest-accepted monthly ₹ recorded + tier contents ranked (feeds OQ9 price band & tier design); else reopen the relevant scope-brief OQ with the founder.

# Appendix — Outreach & booking messages (W38 logistics)

Use the WhatsApp-OK contacts from the W36 field sheet. Send **9:30–11:30 or 17:00–19:00**, never Sunday morning or festival days. Compensation optional `[founder decision]` — if offered, say it upfront: ₹200–500 or a ₹300 recharge for merchants; ₹200 voucher for consumers.

## A. Merchant invite (first message) — bilingual, send as one message
> Namaste [Name]-ji 🙏 I'm [Your name] with [Product] — we're helping [ward/cluster] kirana/repair shops get **more regular customers** via a verified badge, offers & pickup orders (free pilot, no commission).
> I'm doing 25-minute chats with 10 local owners this week to build it right — no selling, just your honest view. Would [Day] [time] or [Day] [time] work? I'll come to your shop. [₹300 thank-you]. — [Your name], [phone]

> हिंदी: नमस्ते [नाम]-जी 🙏 मैं [नाम] हूँ, [प्रोडक्ट] से — हम [क्षेत्र] की किराना/रिपेयर दुकानों को **ज़्यादा पक्के ग्राहक** दिलाने में मदद कर रहे हैं (वेरिफ़ाइड बैज, ऑफ़र, पिकअप ऑर्डर — पायलट मुफ़्त, कोई कमीशन नहीं)। इस हफ़्ते मैं 10 दुकानदारों से 25 मिनट की बातचीत कर रहा हूँ ताकि ऐप सही बने — बेचना नहीं, बस आपकी राय। [दिन] को [समय] या [दिन] को [समय] चलेगा? मैं दुकान पर आऊँगा। [₹300 धन्यवाद-राशि]। — [नाम], [फ़ोन]

## B. Merchant follow-up (no reply in 24h) + confirmation + reminder
- **Follow-up (once):** "Just in case this got lost, [Name]-ji — I'd value 25 minutes of your view this week; shall I come [Day] morning or evening?" (never more than 1 follow-up before field visit)
- **Confirmation:** "Thank you, [Day] [time] at your shop. 3 lines only: (1) your shop & years, (2) one thing you wish you had more of, (3) one registration you're missing/unsure about — or we can cover it live. See you!"
- **Reminder (2h before):** "Namaste [Name]-ji, I'll be at your shop at [time] 🙏 — [Your name], [Product]."

## C. Consumer invite — bilingual
> Hi! I'm [name] with [Product] — a free pilot app where **[city — Bengaluru/Hyderabad] neighbours** find **verified local shops** (real registrations checked), order for pickup & book service slots. Before we launch we want 10 regular shoppers' honest view — 20 min on call/at a café near [area], [₹200 voucher] for your time. Free [Day] [time] or [Day] [time]? — [name], [phone]

> हिंदी: नमस्ते! मैं [नाम] हूँ, [प्रोडक्ट] से — [शहर] के पड़ोसियों के लिए एक मुफ़्त पायलट ऐप जिसमें **वेरिफ़ाइड लोकल दुकानें** (असली रजिस्ट्रेशन चेक होते हैं) दिखती हैं, पिकअप ऑर्डर और सर्विस स्लॉट बुकिंग होती है। लॉन्च से पहले हमें 10 खरीदारों की राय चाहिए — 20 मिनट की बात, [₹200 वाउचर]। [दिन] [समय] या [दिन] [समय]? — [नाम], [फ़ोन]

## 0. One-line model
**SaaS for businesses, free for solo earners.** Registered businesses (XS → L) pay a recurring subscription for the merchant OS (Launch Copilot journeys + renewals calendar + verified badge/storefront/deals). **Individual service providers (rung I) get the verified core at ₹0** — the badge is free because it is the growth engine, and because the ladder monetizes them at graduation, not at onboarding. Consumer side is free = demand engine. **The clean rule:** solo, no premises, no employees = ₹0 (rung I) · any business entity / premises / staff = subscription (XS → L).

## 1. Businesses (XS → L) — DECIDED: SaaS-first subscription
**Decision (founder, 2026-09-08):** recurring subscription paid by business owners; consumer side free. Recorded in `scope-brief.md` "Pricing model" + OQ9. `[ESTIMATED — pending H1–H6/W38 price validation]`

**What the subscription must plausibly contain** (tier candidates; open — tested in Script A §A4 Q14):
1. **Renewals calendar** — never miss a filing/renewal/fine (C5 sticky value; the market's "who reminds me about my next ROC filing?" gap, competitive-map §2.2)
2. **Verified badge presence** (badge + storefront + deals visibility — the growth asset)
3. **Launch Copilot journeys** (setup/registration guidance; possibly the onboarding/landing offer rather than the recurring core)

**Price anchors `[VERIFIED — market-deep-dive.md §D1]`:** billing tools = owners pay **₹500–4,000/yr**; Vyapar mobile ≈ ₹629–1,799/yr; OkCredit free (₹0 anchor); compliance bought as **events at ₹500–15k**. **Anchor resistance to any monthly fee is the core pricing risk.** Interview ladder under test: ₹49 / ₹99 / ₹149 / ₹199 / ₹299 per month (validation-interviews A4 Q13) → feeds the OQ9 price band + tier design.

**Anti-patterns (binding, unchanged):** no per-lead/listing fees (Justdial lesson); never monetize consumers; transparent fees; never a paid look-alike of govt portals.

## 2. Individual providers (rung I) — DECIDED: ₹0 free verified core
**Decision (founder, 2026-09-08):** individuals pay **nothing** — free ProBadge core, no subscription, no commission, no per-job fee. OQ-I3 resolved (was: A free-core / B light-subscription / C order-fee / D verification pass-through).

**Why ₹0 is right (evidence):**
- **They can't be charged profitably yet:** 40% of gig workers earn <₹15k/mo `[VERIFIED]`; the industry's own monetization of individuals — lead fees ₹40–250 + ₹3,000/mo packs + eroding commissions — is hated and drives the "no upfront, outcome-only" new entrants (ProNearMe, HelpRush) `[VERIFIED — I1/I3]`.
- **Free individuals = supply density for cold start.** Solo pros seed bookings in the pilot clusters (marketplace-audit Gap A: per-cluster liquidity) at zero CAC. A ₹0 tier is a *differentiation weapon*, not a giveaway.
- **The ladder monetizes at graduation, not onboarding.** The moment an individual opens a shop / takes premises / hires staff (XS+), the business subscription begins — same profile, upgraded in place (stage-model §0). Free at I is the front door to paid at XS+.
- **No licences to track** → no compliance-calendar product to sell them anyway (ProBadge = L1 identity · L2 background · L3 skill · L4 behaviour `[VERIFIED — individual-verification-trust.md]`).

**What "₹0" includes vs excludes:**
- Includes: ProBadge core (L1 identity via Aadhaar eKYC ≈ ₹0.15/check + L4 order-verified ratings), availability/booking (S14a), order inbox (S14), storefront-of-services, UPI collection, no lead fees ever.
- Excludes (still open): **who pays actual govt/third-party verification fees** for home-entry categories — L2 police verification ₹50–200 (govt) / ₹100–500 (vendor), L3 assessment — **OQ-V2**: lean = govt fee passed through at cost for L2-required categories (beautician/plumbing/AC-at-home per OQ-V1), L1/L4 free; never bundled into a subscription. **₹0 = zero SaaS fee, not necessarily zero govt fee.**

**Boundary rule (no grey zone):** rung I = solo earner, no fixed premises, no employees. Anyone with a business entity / shop premises / employees is XS+ and pays the subscription — including a "shop with mobile service" (XS pays; the individual tech riding that shop's flag stays ₹0 but is covered under the shop's subscription).

**Future (recorded, not promised):** if a paid individual offering ever appears (e.g., promoted placement for pros), it must not resurrect per-lead charges and needs a founder decision — out of scope today.

## 3. Guardrails (binding — business and individual)
Never hold funds (RBI PPI — PA redirect only); **no per-lead charges of any kind, any rung**; never monetize consumers; outcome-linked monetization only where it exists; badge auto-hides on non-compliance (business) and ProBadge stays **chips, never merged** into the compliance badge (LM2); transparent fees; KA Act 2025 + TS Act 2026 obligations respected before any aggregator-style individual matching (OQ-I2, both cities `[VERIFIED 2026-09-08]`).
